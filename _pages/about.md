I am Professor and Head of the [Department of Statistics and Actuarial Science](https://www.sun.ac.za) at Stellenbosch University, where the department teaches postgraduate programmes in Mathematical and Applied Statistics, Actuarial Science, Financial Risk Management, and Data Science. I hold a PhD in Mathematical Statistics from the University of the Free State (2011), and I've been rated a C3 researcher by South Africa's National Research Foundation.

My own research centres on **Bayesian statistics** — non-informative priors, Bayesian machine learning, and variational Bayes — alongside **simulation methods**, **clinical trial methodology**, and industry collaboration. Before moving fully into academia, I spent several years as a biostatistician on multinational Phase III clinical trials, and I still consult on trials and observational studies today. Over my career I've published more than 45 peer-reviewed papers, mostly in biostatistics and epidemiology, and supervised numerous Honours, Masters, and PhD students.

Outside of the department, I referee for several statistics journals, serve as an external examiner and moderator at other South African universities, and I'm a long-standing member of the South African Statistical Association and the International Society for Bayesian Analysis (ISBA).

When I'm not at my desk, I'm usually mountain biking or trail running, raising my twin daughters, or playing music.
